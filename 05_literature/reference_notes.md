Literature Review Notes

1. Lynch, K. M., & Park, F. C. (2017). Modern Robotics.
   Relevance: Core theoretical foundation for the Standard DH convention and Jacobian-based task-space control used in this project.

2. Corke, P. (2023). Robotics, Vision and Control.
   Relevance: Used as the practical implementation guide for the `roboticstoolbox-python` library applied in the simulation.

3. Siciliano, B., et al. (2010). Robotics: Modelling, Planning and Control.
   Relevance: Provided fundamental background on kinematic singularities and operational space control formulations.

4. Chitkara, A., et al. (2019). Damped least squares inverse kinematics.
   Relevance: Inspired the adaptive DLS damping strategy used to stabilize the controller near singular configurations.
